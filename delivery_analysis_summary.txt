Distance vs ETA
----------------
- Correlation between distance and ETA: 0.325
- Mean ETA by distance quintile:
  - Closest trips: 21.66 min
  - Next group: 24.66 min
  - Middle group: 25.65 min
  - Fourth group: 29.99 min
  - Farthest trips: 29.98 min
- Conclusion: Larger distance generally increases ETA.

Traffic Impact
--------------
- Mean delivery times by traffic:
  - jam: 31.15 min
  - high: 27.19 min
  - medium: 26.75 min
  - low: 21.24 min
- The worst traffic condition is jam, which increases delivery time the most.

Weather Impact
--------------
- Mean delivery times by weather:
  - fog: 29.05 min
  - cloudy: 29.02 min
  - windy: 26.21 min
  - stormy: 26.02 min
  - sandstorms: 26.00 min
  - sunny: 21.85 min
- Conclusion: Fog causes the highest delays, with cloudy conditions close behind.

Vehicle Performance
-------------------
- Mean delivery times by vehicle type:
  - electric_scooter: 24.50 min
  - scooter: 24.55 min
  - motorcycle: 27.69 min
- Conclusion: Electric scooters and scooters perform better than motorcycles.

Peak Hour Effect
----------------
- Peak hour deliveries mean ETA: 28.89 min
- Non-peak deliveries mean ETA: 24.03 min
- Peak-hour distance is also higher: 11.29 km vs 8.15 km
- Conclusion: Rush-hour deliveries are roughly 4.9 minutes slower on average and cover longer distances.
